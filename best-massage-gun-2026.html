Best Massage Guns of 2026

8 percussion massagers tested on real muscles. Amplitude, stall force, noise — all measured, not just claimed.

Updated May 2026 • Hands-on testing by athletes and therapists

We've spent months testing massage guns — measuring actual amplitude with calipers, testing stall force with a scale, recording noise levels with a decibel meter, and most importantly, using them on sore muscles after workouts. The difference between a great massage gun and a mediocre one is immediately obvious when you're trying to work out a knot in your trap.

Here are the 8 best massage guns you can buy in 2026, ranked by overall performance and value.

#1 Best Overall

Theragun PRO Plus

★★★★★ 4.8/5
Price: $399–$449

The Theragun PRO Plus remains the benchmark that every other massage gun is measured against. The 16mm amplitude means it reaches deeper tissue than any competitor — you can feel the difference immediately on thick muscle groups like quads and glutes. The 60 lbs of stall force means it won't bog down no matter how hard you press.

The triangular ergonomic grip is Theragun's secret weapon. It lets you reach your own back, shoulders, and hamstrings without awkward contortions. The rotating arm adds even more angles. The Therabody app provides guided routines for specific activities (post-run, desk worker, pre-game), which are surprisingly well-designed.

Is it worth $400+? If you train seriously or deal with chronic muscle tension, absolutely. The depth and power difference over $150 guns is not subtle.

Pros

  • Industry-leading 16mm amplitude
  • 60 lbs stall force — never stalls
  • Ergonomic multi-grip design
  • Rotating arm for hard-to-reach areas
  • Bluetooth app with guided routines
  • OLED screen with force meter

Cons

#2 Best Quiet

Hypervolt 2 Pro

★★★★★ 4.7/5
Price: $249–$299

Hyperice engineered the Hypervolt 2 Pro to be the quietest powerful massage gun on the market, and they succeeded. At speed 3 (the most commonly used setting), it registers just 48 dB — quieter than a normal conversation. You can use it in a shared office, while watching TV, or at 6 AM without waking anyone up.

But quiet doesn't mean weak. The 14mm amplitude and 40 lbs of stall force handle most muscle groups effectively. The pressure sensor ring around the head glows different colors based on how hard you're pressing — green for light, blue for medium, red for heavy — which helps you maintain consistent pressure.

The 3-hour battery life is excellent, and at 1.8 lbs it's noticeably lighter than the Theragun PRO. If you value quiet operation and don't need the absolute deepest percussion, this is the one.

Pros

  • Whisper-quiet (48 dB at speed 3)
  • Pressure sensor with LED feedback
  • 3-hour battery life
  • Lightweight at 1.8 lbs
  • 5 speed settings
  • Premium build quality

Cons

  • 14mm amplitude (less deep than Theragun)
  • 40 lbs stall force (adequate but not elite)
  • No rotating arm — harder to reach back
  • Non-removable battery

#3 Best Value

Ekrin Athletics B37S

★★★★☆ 4.6/5
Price: $160–$200

The Ekrin B37S is the massage gun we recommend most often because it delivers 90% of the premium experience at half the price. The 56 lbs of stall force actually exceeds the Hypervolt 2 Pro, and the 6+ hour battery life is the longest we've tested in any massage gun, period.

The 12mm amplitude is the one area where it falls short of premium options — you won't get quite as deep into thick muscle tissue. But for most people's needs (general soreness, post-workout recovery, desk worker tension), 12mm is plenty. The all-metal internal gearing means this thing will last years without degrading.

Ekrin also offers a lifetime warranty, which is almost unheard of in this category. At $160-200, it's the best value in massage guns right now.
